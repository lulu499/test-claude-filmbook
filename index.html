What the video does

Measured frame by frame from the 1080p file. Everything happens on one black stage. Images sit in cream-bordered cards that are wider than they are tall, and a dot-matrix screen texture covers the whole picture. Pixel values below are at 1920×1080. Frame counts are at 23.976 fps.

Stage

Near-black (RGB 7,7,7 in corners) with slow grey smoke in the lower third and along the right edge. Two small star flares sit on the edges, one top at x≈575 and one bottom-right at x≈1765, bluish-white with a warm fringe.

Card border

A 1px dark outline, then about 3px of cream (≈238,228,211), then a 1–2px cool grey inner line before the image. This reads as chromatic fringing. A soft cream halo spreads about 15px outside and breaks into dots through the screen texture.

Card sizes

Mostly 2.39:1 “scope” cards, 1100–1540px wide and centred. Also 1.85:1 cards, pairs of tall portrait cards about 0.62:1, and single concept-art cards about 0.9:1.

Ghost stack

On a stack cut, the old card moves behind the new one, scaled to 0.957, shifted +71px right and −50px up. The front card is about 90% opaque, so the ghost’s edge shows through. The stack reaches three deep near the end.

Red tags

Flat red boxes (≈172,64,53) holding cream Chakobsa-style glyphs with a dark red drop shadow. One-line titles sit centred on the bottom edge. Paragraph tags hang off the bottom-right, top-left or right side.

Motion while holding

Cards never sit still. They drift a few pixels and slowly scale by about ±4% over a 3–6 second hold. Source clips also move inside the card. Stills here get a gentle push-in instead.
