What the video does

These are frame-accurate measurements from the 1080p file: card edges tracked on every one of its 2,637 frames, FFTs of the textures, and per-pixel border profiles. Values are in pixels at 1920×1080. Frame counts are at 23.976 fps.

Hold motion

While a shot holds, everything shrinks linearly by about 2.2% per second toward the exact centre of the frame (959, 539). It never grows and never drifts sideways. This held in all 30 measured holds.

LED mesh

Each card shows a dot grid attached to the card: always 300 cells across its width, so the pitch shrinks with the card (4.36px at 1308 wide, 3.85px at 1154). The grid runs through the border and the glow, which makes the edges look dotted.

Background

One static cloud plate for the whole video. It's near-black (7,7,7) with a smoke bank along the bottom rising up the right edge, covered by a fixed 3px screen grid. There are two constant, non-twinkling flares and a few dust specks drifting at 3–5px per second.

Border

A single cream stroke about 3.6px wide. Colour fringing (blue shifted down-right) gives an amber edge on the top-left and a cool edge on the bottom-right. There's an 8–10px inner glow and a faint warm outer glow biased up-left. Blacks sit at about 9, highlights top out around 240.

Pushes

Consecutive shots sit one full screen apart. A push pans the camera 1,900px sideways or 1,060px vertically. Its position curve is a Laplace CDF: speed grows ×1.5 per frame, peaks at about 300px per frame, then decays ×0.68 per frame. It takes about 26 frames, or 1.1 s.

Depth moves

Zoom-through: the old card shrinks faster and faster to 90%, cuts at the midpoint, and the new card lands at 117% and decays. Slam: the new card starts at about 190% (full-bleed) and decays ×0.8 per frame. Power-on is a slam with one blank frame, and power-off goes on, half, off, on, half, off.

Ghost stack

On a stack cut the old card stays exactly where it is. It powers down to a grey-cream with a stronger dot grid, and its clip keeps playing. The new card lands about 4% bigger, offset (−78, +52), and is slightly see-through. The stack reaches three deep.

Tags

Dark red boxes, about 92% opaque, holding an embossed Coptic-style glyph script. Title tags are 52px tall. Paragraph tags open with a quoted title line and have 25px line spacing. They appear with their card, with no animation.

Flicker

Even the still concept art varies in brightness by ±2–3% from frame to frame, so there's a quiet projector flicker on every card.
